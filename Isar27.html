Small Surplus — Built to Grow Without Getting Fluffy
NUTRITION PROGRAM
🎯 The Strategy
  • Very small surplus overall — enough to support muscle growth without adding unnecessary fat.
  • Protein stays fixed at 140g both training and rest days — plenty for muscle growth, no need for more.
  • Carbs are the lever: higher on training days to fuel legs, slightly lower on rest days.
🏋️ Training Days (5x/week) — 2,250 kcal
  • Protein: 140g
  • Carbs: 285g — most people trying to grow their legs don't eat enough of these
  • Fat: 60g — enough for hormones while leaving room for carbs
🛌 Rest Days (2x/week) — 2,050 kcal
  • Protein: 140g
  • Carbs: 235g
  • Fat: 65g
⏱ 60–90 Min Before Lifting
  • 30–60g carbs · 20–30g protein · low fat
  • Rice + chicken
  • Greek yogurt + cereal
  • Bagel + turkey
🍽️ Within ~2 Hrs After Training
  • 30–40g protein · 60–80g carbs
  • Rice + chicken
  • Potatoes + steak
  • Protein shake + cereal if in a hurry

💊 Supplements — Priority Order
  • Creatine monohydrate: 5g daily

Steps Over Structured Cardio — For Now
CARDIO PROGRAM
🚶 Current Approach: No Traditional Cardio Needed
  • Already spending a lot of time walking around the gym and coaching clients — that counts.
  • Target 8,000–10,000 steps/day instead of structured cardio sessions.
  • Enough to keep cardiovascular health up without interfering with leg growth.
📈 If Fat Gain Starts Outpacing the Goal
  • Add 2 sessions/week, 20 minutes each
  • Incline treadmill, easy pace — should still be able to hold a conversation
  • No HIIT needed for the current goal
Rest, Repair & Manage Training Stress
RECOVERY PROGRAM
😴
Sleep 8–9 Hours
Daily non-negotiable. This is when growth hormone peaks and muscle tissue actually repairs.
💧
2.7–3.5L Water Daily
More on hard training days or when sweating heavily.
🥩
Protein at 4–5 Meals
About 25–35g per meal spread throughout the day, not one big serving.
🚶
8,000–10,000 Steps
Daily movement target, separate from structured training.
🧘
5–10 Min Mobility Daily
Hip, ankle, and thoracic mobility work to keep training patterns clean.
🔥
Before Every Workout
Dynamic warm-up, then 2–3 ramp-up sets before the first heavy lift. Never jump straight to working weight.
🌬️
After Every Workout — ~5 Min
Hip flexor stretch, hamstring stretch, glute stretch, calf stretch, and deep breathing to bring heart rate down. No need for long static stretching sessions.
📆
2 Full Rest Days/Week
Non-negotiable — this is when the muscle trained during the week actually adapts and grows.
Training Management
01
Progressive Overload — Pick One Per Week
Add 5lb, add 1 rep, improve technique, slow the eccentric, or increase range of motion. Don't try to increase everything at once — pick one lever per exercise per week.
02
Leave Reps in Reserve
Avoid taking every set to absolute failure — leave 0–2 reps in reserve on most sets, and save true failure for the last set of isolation exercises.
03
Deload Trigger
If feeling unusually fatigued, lifts drop for a week or two, or constantly sore — reduce working sets by about 30–40% for one week, then build back up.

Programmed Set Volume By Muscle Group
WEEKLY VOLUME
🎯 Why This Split Is Built This Way
  • Glutes sit at ~15 quality sets/week — enough to maintain and slowly grow, not overload.
  • Quads get 16 direct sets/week to bring them up toward glute development.
  • Calves get 11 sets/week across three separate days.
  • Lateral delts get 6 direct sets plus pressing volume; triceps get 9 direct sets plus pressing volume.
  • Compound lifts get 2–3 min rest, isolation work gets 60–90 sec — matched to the recovery demand of each.

🔮 One Thing to Eventually Change
  • Consider replacing one hip-thrust-focused day with a lengthened-position glute movement — a 45° hyperextension or cable kickback.
  • Hip thrusts are fantastic, but relying on them as the main glute exercise multiple days every week isn't the most balanced long-term approach.
  • Day 1 already includes a 45° back extension (glute bias) — the next iteration could shift Day 5's hip thrust burnout toward a similar lengthened-position movement.
✅ Overall Takeaway
  • This program is aligned with the goal: balanced lower body, maintaining/slowly growing glutes, while bringing up quads, calves, lateral delts, and triceps.
